How the checker works

The checker compares each course file with the department's model course file (USS001): the seven checklist folders (columns 1–7), current and previous syllabus, and for every assessment the instrument, the answer key or rubric, and graded High, Medium and Low samples. Student work is counted, never listed. Quality of content is judged by the reviewer in the comments.

Department rules: graded samples per assessment and per level (High, Medium, Low) are 1 for classes under 30 students, 2 for 30 to 60 and 3 for more than 60. The syllabus must state the section and campus. Empty course files are fine until their due date.
